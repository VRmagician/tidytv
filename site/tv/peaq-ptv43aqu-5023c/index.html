Debloat the PEAQ PTV 43AQU-5023C

Android TV 11 · 15 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This Changhong-built PEAQ set sells under the OK. AI PONT name and shares its Homwee vendor layer with other Changhong sets. It ships with telemetry, ad and recommendation rows on the home screen, and preloaded vendor apps. Each app on the list has its own switch in the app, so you decide what goes.

It was tested on one set in 2026, the same day and through one restart. Every app stayed off, and Live TV, the HDMI inputs, YouTube, Netflix and Spotify all played. The remote's Netflix, YouTube, Input and Settings buttons still worked. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.
