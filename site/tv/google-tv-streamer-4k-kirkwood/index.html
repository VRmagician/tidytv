Debloat the Google TV Streamer

Google TV 14 · 11 apps on the list · tested on a real set

The Google TV Streamer ships with Google telemetry, feedback and ads, four Privacy Sandbox modules and preloaded apps. The list is short, at 11 apps. It is a dongle, so it has no Live TV app and no HDMI inputs, and it keeps Google's home screen choice open.

Casting reads its device credentials from the setup wizard, so turning that off kills casting on this device, as on Google's own Chromecast. TidyTV keeps that in mind. It turns the rest off from your phone with a backup first and deletes nothing. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

What always stays on

  • The setup wizard, which holds the cast credentials
  • Casting
  • Google Play Store and Play Services
  • The remote and Bluetooth

Good to know on this model

  • Turning off the setup wizard kills casting with an Untrusted device error. Turn it back on and wait for the cast service to restart.
  • The debugging port stays closed until you pair once through Wireless debugging.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will casting still work on my Google TV Streamer?

Yes, as long as the setup wizard stays on. It holds the credentials that casting needs.

Will it remove ads?

It turns off Google's telemetry, feedback and ad services and a few preloaded apps. The list is short, at 11 apps.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns the apps back on and puts the Google TV home screen back in one tap.
