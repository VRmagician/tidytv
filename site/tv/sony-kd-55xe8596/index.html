Debloat the Sony BRAVIA KD-55XE8596

Android TV 9 · 28 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This 2017 UK Sony set runs Android TV 9 and Sony has stopped updating it. It ships with a viewing tracker that identifies what is on screen, Sony and Google content, ads and promos, a retail demo, telemetry and remote support, and hotel and business mode.

Home, Netflix, Jellyfin, Live TV and the HDMI inputs all kept working after every batch, and no new crashes appeared. It has not been lived with for days or checked across a restart. TidyTV turns these off from your phone over Wi-Fi, saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

Good to know on this model

  • Live TV on this UK set hands over to YouView. Open the Sony TV app, not YouView, and keep both on.
  • The home screen can ignore a new home screen setting until the stock one is switched off.
  • The Internet Browser tile is the only part of the HbbTV layer that is hidden.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the red button still work?

Yes. The HbbTV layer is kept, and only its Internet Browser tile is hidden.

Will the voice button and casting still work?

Yes. Google Assistant, Chromecast, mirroring and DLNA are kept on this set.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back in one tap.
