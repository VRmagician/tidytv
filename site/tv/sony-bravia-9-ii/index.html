What always stays on

  • HDMI inputs, the analog input and the Live TV app
  • The IR blaster and its setup service
  • The voice assistant and Gemini, which keep working without the stock home screen
  • AirPlay, Sony firmware updates and all streaming apps
  • The BRAVIA CAM sensing service

Good to know on this model

  • The Input key on the remote opens Sony's own bottom panel. It still works with the new home screen.
  • USB debugging alone does not open a network connection on this set, so turn on Wireless debugging for the first connection.
  • The TV has a hardware microphone switch. If it says the microphone is blocked, that switch is the cause, not this list.
  • If Wi-Fi was set up in the first-run wizard, switching the setup wizard off makes the TV forget that network. Join the network again in Settings first, or use Ethernet.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the voice button still work on my Sony BRAVIA 9 II?

Yes. Voice and Gemini are kept on purpose and keep working. The Gemini tab on the stock home screen goes away with that home screen.

Does casting still work?

AirPlay is kept, so iPhones and iPads can still send to the TV. Chromecast built-in is one of the optional apps the list switches off, and you can switch it back on in the app.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back, in one tap.
