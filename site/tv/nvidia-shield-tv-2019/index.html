Debloat the NVIDIA SHIELD TV 2019

Android TV 11 · 34 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This SHIELD ships with NVIDIA usage statistics, crash reports and a feedback form, plus sponsored rows and partner app installs on the home screen. It also has the NVIDIA Games app, a network storage and file sharing service, a Google data-saver service, and several factory test and beta tools that a home owner does not need.

The list was run on a 2019 SHIELD TV on SHIELD Experience 9.2.4, and the owner chose to leave firmware updates off. A user who ran it posted a screenshot of the working setup, but it has not been checked on other units. TidyTV needs Network debugging turned on once. After that it turns the list off from your phone over Wi-Fi, with a backup first and nothing deleted.

What always stays on

  • Netflix, YouTube and Prime Video, which stay installed
  • Google Assistant, its hotword and the on-device speech service behind the mic button
  • Alexa and Google Home control, and the SHIELD phone remote app
  • Chromecast built-in
  • Bluetooth pairing, controller mapping and the infrared remote services

Good to know on this model

  • Firmware updates are switched off by this list, along with NVIDIA's update helpers. NVIDIA still sends security patches, so switch the updates back on before you contact NVIDIA support or if you want those fixes.
  • A SHIELD Experience update can switch some of these apps back on. Run TidyTV again after one.
  • Ambient mode (Backdrop) and Live Channels are switched off. If your screensaver is set to Backdrop, choose another one first, or the screensaver will stop showing.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the voice button still work on my NVIDIA SHIELD TV 2019?

Yes. Google Assistant, its hotword and the speech service behind the mic button are kept, and Alexa and Google Home control stay on.

Does casting still work?

Yes. Chromecast built-in and the SHIELD phone remote app are kept on purpose.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back, in one tap.
