Good to know on this model

  • Wait about 30 seconds after a change before restarting the TV. A restart too soon can bring back an app that was just turned off.
  • Sony's application disabler turns some apps back on at every start, including the AirPlay app and some Sony IoT apps. Leave them as they are.
  • The TV's IP address often changes after a restart. Reserve one in your router so your phone can find the TV.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the voice button still work on my Sony BRAVIA BF1?

Yes. Voice search is kept on purpose, and Google Assistant is an optional entry, so it stays on.

Does casting still work?

Yes. AirPlay and Chromecast are kept on this list.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns the apps back on and puts the old home screen back, in one tap. A factory reset also turns every app back on.
