Debloat the Chromecast with Google TV 4K

Google TV 14 · 15 apps on the list · tested on a real set

The Chromecast ships with feedback and remote-usage logging, network diagnostics reporting and Google's Privacy Sandbox ad services. It also carries preloaded apps such as Google TV and Play Movies, a print spooler, and a Google ambient screensaver. Each one has its own switch in the app.

The list was applied to one unit. Everything kept on purpose still worked afterwards, including Google Assistant, Cast, the phone remote, the Play Store and Bluetooth. TidyTV turns these items off from your phone over Wi-Fi. It saves a backup first, switches each app off without deleting it, and stops if anything looks wrong.

What always stays on

  • Google Assistant and voice search.

  • The phone remote service.
  • The Play Store and Play Services.
  • Gboard, the on-screen keyboard.
  • Bluetooth.
  • Remote firmware updates.

Good to know on this model

  • The remote's YouTube button does nothing once the stock home screen is off. Fixing it needs a free key-remapping app and some one-time setup that this page does not cover.
  • Google's Privacy Sandbox ad services are off in the tested set. If the Play Store or Assistant misbehave, turn them back on with their switch.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the voice button still work on my Chromecast with Google TV 4K?

Yes. Google Assistant and voice search are kept on, so the voice button keeps working.

Does casting still work?

Yes. The Cast receiver is kept on, so phone casting works.

Can I get the old home screen back?

Yes. Restore everything turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back in one tap.
