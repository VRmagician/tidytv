Debloat the TCL BeyondTV (Poland)

Android TV 9 · 26 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This Polish TCL runs Android TV 9 and ships with TCL analytics, a bug report uploader, partner app attribution and a partner app auto installer. The home screen carries recommendation rows, T-Channel and TCL promo rows, and TCL adds a message and promo box and retail demo stickers. A TV Guard cleaner and a TCL app store are also listed.

The list was applied in groups with a screenshot after each, then rebooted once. Home, the Inputs menu, the remote's Source and Settings buttons and Netflix worked. It has not been lived with for days. TidyTV saves a backup first and never deletes anything.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Will the voice button still work on my TCL BeyondTV?

Not with the default list. It turns off Google voice search and the mic button, plus TCL's Assistant bridge. Switch them back on in the app if you use voice.

Does casting still work?

Miracast and TCL's T-Cast phone link are on the list and are switched off. Switch them back on if you use them.

Will it stop the ads on the home screen?

It turns off the recommendation rows, the T-Channel and promo rows and the TCL message and promo box.
