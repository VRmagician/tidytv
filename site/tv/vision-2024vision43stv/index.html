Debloat the Vision 2024VISION43STV

Android 13 · 16 apps on the list · tested on a real set

This Vision TV runs CVTE's own home screen instead of Google TV, and ships with ads and telemetry, manufacturer content and ad services, and a skinned stock launcher. The list covers those and a few apps off by the tested owner's choice.

It was tested on one set for a day, and the clean home screen part is only half done. The TV has no Developer options, so the connection comes from a factory menu code on the remote. Good to know on this model

  • Developer options are not reachable. Turn on ADB from the factory menu with the remote, and do not touch factory reset, aging or panel items.
  • The build fingerprint is spoofed, so match by board name.

How to do it

  1. Turn on developer options On the TV, open Settings › System › About and select the build number 7 times. Then turn on USB debugging, and Network debugging if you see it.
  2. Tap your TV in TidyTV Your phone finds the TV on the Wi-Fi. Tick "Always allow" and press Allow when the TV asks.
  3. Review, then tidy TidyTV saves a backup, shows these apps with switches, and turns off the ones you pick, checking each one.

Questions

Can I get the old home screen back on my Vision 2024VISION43STV?

Yes. Restore everything in the app turns every app back on and puts the old home screen back in one tap. TidyTV only switches apps off, so nothing is ever deleted.

How do I turn on debugging on this TV?

Switch to a source, then on the remote press MENU 1 1 4 7 quickly, and choose DEBUG, ADB ENABLE, ON. Do not touch the factory reset items in that menu.

Does TidyTV delete anything on my Vision 2024VISION43STV?

No. It turns apps off with Android's own setting, saves a backup first and checks each one. You can turn any app back on with its switch.
