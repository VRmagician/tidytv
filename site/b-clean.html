Questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. tidytv is free and open source under the MIT licence. No ads, no account, no tracking.

Do I need a computer or root?

No. Your phone and TV only need to be on the same Wi-Fi. Most TVs just ask "Allow debugging?" once.

Which TVs work?

Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes from TCL, Sony, Xiaomi, Philips, Hisense, Nvidia, Chromecast with Google TV and more. Fire TV gets the clean-up but keeps Amazon's home screen. Samsung and LG don't run Android.

Will it remove YouTube ads?

No. It turns off the TV maker's ads, sponsored rows and trackers. Ads inside apps are not affected.

Does it survive a Google TV update?

Turned-off apps normally stay off. If an update turns something back on, run tidytv again; your backup and undo list carry over.

Should I turn debugging off afterwards?

Yes. tidytv shows you how, then checks the TV no longer accepts debugging connections.
