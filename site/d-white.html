Good questions.

Will this break my TV?

It never uninstalls anything and won't touch the apps a TV needs. A backup comes first, and Restore turns every app back on.

Do I need a computer?

No. Your phone and TV only need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

Is it really free?

Yes. Free and open source under the MIT licence. No ads, no account, no tracking.

Which TVs work?

Android TV and Google TV. Fire TV gets the clean-up but keeps Amazon's home screen. Samsung and LG don't run Android.

Will it remove YouTube ads?

No. It turns off the TV maker's ads and trackers. Ads inside apps are not affected.

Should I turn debugging off after?

Yes. tidytv shows you how, then checks the TV no longer accepts debugging connections.
